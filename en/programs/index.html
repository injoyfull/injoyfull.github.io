  • Session 2

    Hanbok Design touch · sight

    This session visits a studio where hanbok — traditional Korean clothing — is made. Children watch the makers’ hands up close and touch real hanbok fabric. Stroke it along the grain and it rustles; turn it toward the light and the color shifts. With the fabric they choose, they layer, wrap and glue something of their own.

  • Session 1

    House Design touch · balance

    With a mission card — “three things a house needs” — children head outside to collect what could become walls, a roof and a garden. Using clay like glue, they join it all together, and once the roof is on, it’s a house of their own. Building next to a friend’s house is welcome too.

    Tue Oct 6 · Thu Oct 8 · We head outdoors · Taken home that day or at the next session, depending on the clay

    Book →
  • Session 2

    Maze Design movement · space

    Outside, children choose twigs, stones and seeds. They stand them up as walls inside a wooden tray, draw patterns with acrylic markers, then roll a marble through. The child decides where the path goes — and rebuilds it when it gets stuck.

    Tue Oct 13 · Thu Oct 15 · We head outdoors

    Book →
  • Session 3

    Character Design touch · imagination

    With a chewy clay that hardens like stone, children make a character to live with them — and, from the same clay, a spot for it to sit. Once hardened, it becomes a little object to keep at home.

    Tue Oct 20 · Thu Oct 22 · It needs time to harden, so it goes home at the next session

    Book →
  • Session 4

    Canvas Design texture · sight

    Shiny, fluffy, rough, see-through. Children feel and choose fabrics, then weave them over and under across a blank canvas, filling it the way it feels to them. Even the loose ends become part of the work.

    Tue Oct 27 · Thu Oct 29 · One piece for the wall, taken home that day

CREA:D PASS — your own studio pass

Book and visit — the way you would a kids’ café or a LEGO room — and the in:JOYFULL studio becomes your child’s personal workshop.

Once children start elementary school, their days fill up with after-school academies. Without chances to discover the artist inside themselves, their sense switches go off one by one. CREA:D PASS is time for children to switch them back on, themselves.

in:JOYFULL is a hands-on maker studio. With all kinds of open-ended materials (loose parts), children choose what they want to do today.

  • Claymaking with clay
  • Sand bottlesdecorating sand bottles
  • Collagecollage · mixed media
  • CodingLEGO · Cubroid coding
  • Makerwoodwork · glue gun

Within the activity they choose, they use materials and tools freely — observing, asking and deciding for themselves. For that hour, each child becomes the planner, developer and researcher of their own work.
