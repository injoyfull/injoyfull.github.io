Drop 01 · September

September: Designing Fall

In all four sessions, the children do the designing

  • Session 1

    Stone Design touch

    Children feel a basket full of stones and choose one stone of their own. Choosing is already design. Then they draw their own world on it with acrylic markers.

    Tue Sep 1 · Wed Sep 2 · Thu Sep 3

    Book on Naver →
  • Session 2

    Hanbok Design touch · sight

    This session visits a studio where hanbok — traditional Korean clothing — is made. Children watch the makers’ hands up close and touch real hanbok fabric. Stroke it along the grain and it rustles; turn it toward the light and the color shifts. With the fabric they choose, they layer, wrap and glue something of their own.

    in:JOYFULL × Yeonwoorang Hanbok

    Tue Sep 8 · Wed Sep 9 · Thu Sep 10 · We meet at the Yeonwoorang Hanbok studio (Misa, Hanam)
    Same fee as the other sessions

    Book on Naver →
  • Session 3

    Rabbit Design color · chance

    No brushes. Pour, tilt, and wait. The child chooses which colors to pour, but no one decides where they’ll flow. The child names the new colors that appear.

    Tue Sep 15 · Wed Sep 16 · Thu Sep 17 · It needs time to dry, so children take it home at the next session

    Book on Naver →
  • Session 4

    Shadow Design light · exploration

    Children search for and choose what to place: cut paper, beads, leaves picked up outside. Set under sunlight, the shapes of the objects become shadows — and the shadows become the design.

    Tue Sep 22 · Wed Sep 23 · Thu Oct 1

    Book on Naver →

Good to know

No finished examples, no set order

  • Is it okay to be new?Yes. Every session starts fresh, so whichever week you come is a beginning.
  • Do we bring anything?Just yourself. Materials are ready for each person. Comfortable clothes that can get a little paint on them are all you need.
  • What happens to what they make?It goes up on the wall that day and becomes part of an exhibition that grows all season — or they can hug it and take it home.
