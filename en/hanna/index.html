The path so far걸어온 길

Senses and making, coding and language, and design.
The paths I’ve walked came together as in:JOYFULL.

I’m Hanna Lee, founder of in:JOYFULL, a creative studio in Hanam, Korea. I design and lead creative programs for children and adults, teach creative coding, and work as a graphic designer.

In my first year of high school, I sat in a classroom in a foreign country, trying to follow lessons in clumsy English. My teachers waited for me, and that’s where I first learned it’s okay to be slow. I studied advertising and graphic design, then came home to a Korea that still wanted quick right answers.

Holding my fourth baby, I read a book at the postnatal care center and met the word “coding” for the first time. Meeting children through that language, I realized that children are creative to begin with — we just don’t often give them the chance to bring it out. During COVID, I turned one room at home into an art room and found my daughters spreading out their ideas freely. After that, for shy children, art became a way to get closer to people, and I watched them start telling their own stories. It made me think: every child has their own pace, so this should be a safe room that can wait for them. That room grew into in:JOYFULL.

A place that values process over finished work, and each person’s pace over right answers. I find something to look forward to here every day, too.
