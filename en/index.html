Full of JOY — I'm oli_hanna.

I was a child who was afraid to express herself — always sure everyone else could do it better, keeping what I wanted to draw locked inside.

Holding my fourth child, I asked myself: how should I raise them? The answer was to grow alongside them, not to teach at them. The little art room I made with my daughters became in:JOYFULL — three years now in Hanam.

I worked as a visual designer, lived abroad for years, and came home to raise four children — a colorful life I now unfold, piece by piece, at in:JOYFULL. This is my own studio, too.

A child once burst into tears when a friend's elbow smudged her drawing. Then one day she said, all by herself: "I can just change the paper!" I hand children many small experiences and wait while their strength grows — designing an environment where they can dare one small brave thing at a time.

And I offer the same 90 minutes to parents. The adults' seat is always open.
