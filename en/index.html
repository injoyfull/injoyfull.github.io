What we do

A studio where people make their own answers

What in:JOYFULL is

A creative studio with drawers full of open-ended materials — clay, paper, thread, stones, bottle caps — alongside coding robots and building blocks. We don’t hand out samples or step-by-step instructions. People choose their materials, follow their own idea, and talk about what they made.

Who it’s for

Kids POP — weekly 90-minute sessions for ages 5–7.
CREA:D PASS — a bookable open-studio hour for school-age children.
Creative Club ’90 — Friday mornings for adults, opening soon.

Who runs it

Hanna Lee (oli_hanna), founder. Trained in advertising and graphic design in North Carolina, USA; taught coding to 800+ students; mother of four. She also designs creative programs for schools and public institutions in Gyeonggi-do.

oli_hanna

Full of joy — I’m Hanna.

We live with more input than ever, yet there are few places to bring our own thoughts out. in:JOYFULL offers exactly that: time and space to express something of your own. Instead of right answers — 90 minutes with no right answers.

Many materials, a free space, and ideas that mix together. When you keep choosing your own materials and expressing your own thoughts, you slowly become someone who designs your own life. Isn’t that something to look forward to?

Just as countless experiences made me who I am, I hope in:JOYFULL becomes one of those experiences for others. Living in several countries, raising four children, and doing many different kinds of work taught me that this kind of strength grows from the environment. A space full of joy — my research lab and everyone’s studio — is that environment.

Pop! Time for thoughts to become pictures and words. I can’t wait to hear your story. See you at in:JOYFULL.
